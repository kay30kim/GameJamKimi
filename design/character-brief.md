
# Kimi the Bunny — Character Brief

## Character Overview
Kimi is the friendly mascot and storytelling companion
of KimiTales. Kimi guides young children through
interactive stories that teach positive life lessons.

## Appearance
- Warm cream-coloured fur
- Long bunny ears
- Soft peach-pink inner ears
- Large expressive dark brown eyes
- Small rounded body and soft proportions
- Small peach-coloured scarf

## Personality
- Curious and adventurous
- Kind and empathetic
- Patient and encouraging
- Playful and friendly
- Never judgmental or intimidating

## Expressions
1. Happy
2. Curious
3. Surprised
4. Sad
5. Excited

## Behaviour
- Encourages children to explore and make choices.
- Celebrates effort and learning.
- Uses simple, age-appropriate language.
- Never shames children for incorrect answers.
- Reacts naturally to the child's decisions.

## Visual Style
- Soft 2D storybook illustration
- Rounded shapes and proportions
- Warm pastel colour palette
- Simple, recognizable expressions
- Consistent appearance across all scenes
