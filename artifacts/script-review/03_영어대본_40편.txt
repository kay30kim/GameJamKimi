--- 장면 1. Different wants and paces ---
[해설] The friends gather for a snack. Grandma holds the cookies. Each friend notices how their body feels.
[행동·촬영] All three sit facing one another. Grandma holds the plate.
  Alice: I'd like a cookie, and I want my turn soon.
  Momo: I'd like a little, then some time to rest.
  Grandma Owl: I'm glad you told me. You don't need the same amount or pace.

--- 장면 2. A request about turns ---
[해설] Alice thinks knowing when her turn comes would make waiting easier.
[행동·촬영] Stay seated for dialogue. Keep bodies separated from the plate.
  Alice: Please tell me when it's my turn.
  Alice: “제 차례가 되면 알려 주세요” means “Please tell me when it's my turn” in English.
  Grandma Owl: Yes. Then we can both know who is ready to receive it.
  Momo: I feel comfortable when my pace is respected too.

--- 장면 3. How shall I handle waiting? ---
[해설] Grandma holds the plate. Alice chooses how to wait.
[행동·촬영] Confirm the turn in words before standing within reach. Pass on 'Here you are', then thank the giver.
  Alice: Shall I explain that I'm eager for my turn?

[선택 A] Ask to be told, then wait
[선택 결과 해설] Alice looks at Grandma, ready to ask.
[행동·촬영] After Grandma names Alice's turn, both stand within reach. Pass on 'Here you are.' Alice thanks her after receipt, then both sit.
  Alice: Please tell me when it's my turn. I'll wait.
  Grandma Owl: It's your turn now. Here you are.
  Alice: Thank you. Knowing when I can receive it helps me feel comfortable.
[다음] 이 선택의 결과가 끝난 뒤 장면 4로 이어집니다.

[선택 B] Approach the plate before asking
[선택 결과 해설] Alice stands in a hurry. Grandma's plate is still out of reach.
[행동·촬영] Show haste through walking and expression. Keep the plate with Grandma; stop for her line. After the repaired request and turn confirmation, stand within reach and pass on 'Here you are.' Sit again.
  Grandma Owl: A moment. Could you ask first? I'm still holding the plate.
  Alice: I reached because I wanted a turn quickly. Please tell me when it's my turn.
  Grandma Owl: I understand. It is your turn now. Here you are.
  Alice: Thank you. Even after hurrying, I can try asking again.
[다음] 이 선택의 결과가 끝난 뒤 장면 4로 이어집니다.

--- 장면 4. Listen to our bodies too ---
[해설] Alice has chewed and swallowed. Momo would like a little now.
[행동·촬영] Eating is narrated only; the food does not disappear. Alice and Momo stand within reach. Pass on 'Here you are', then let the receiver answer.
  Momo: I'd like a little. Could you pass the plate?
  Alice: Yes. I've swallowed, so I can talk. Here you are.
  Momo: Thank you. Choosing my amount and pace felt comfortable. I enjoy being together.
  Alice: I'm happy you're happy. Let's check with each other next time.
  Grandma Owl: If you're full or a food feels uncomfortable, you can stop or ask for something else.
  Alice: Shall we say “Please tell me when it's my turn”? Speak after swallowing, or just listen.
